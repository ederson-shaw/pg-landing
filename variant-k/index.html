MANAGER COACHING REPORT™

You don’t need another call score.
You need to know what the rep keeps missing.

PitchGenius turns buyer movement, call evidence, and seller behavior into patterns a manager can actually coach.

WHAT CHANGED

The buyer moved from evaluating to prioritizing internal alignment. Stakeholders are now coordinating next steps.

WHAT THE REP SAW

The rep saw interest and agreement. They believed the value was clear and timing was aligned.

WHAT THE REP MISSED

The buyer expressed concerns about internal bandwidth, implementation risk, and executive buy-in. These signals were softened and went unchallenged.

WHAT BLOCKED MOVEMENT

Unanswered risk and process questions created hesitation. No clear next step or success plan was agreed to.

WHAT TO COACH

Help the rep slow down, explore concerns, and co-create the path forward with the buyer.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

The buyer will take this back internally. Without stronger alignment, the next conversation will reset or stall.

RECURRING COACHING PATTERNS✓ Pushing too early✓ Mistaking politeness for commitment✓ Explaining instead of exploring✓ Avoiding stakeholder questions✓ Missing perceived risk✓ Failing to equip the champion
