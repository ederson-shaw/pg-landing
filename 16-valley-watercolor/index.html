Questions a rep asks before trusting this.

Does this score me and hand the score to my manager?

No. Your calibration answers — the ten questions that tune how the co-pilot talks to you — are private. Managers see usage and deal activity. They do not see your answers.

Is this replacing me?

No. PitchGenius makes you better at the human part of selling. It tells you what to say; you say it. The rep is the hero. The co-pilot is the earpiece.

How is this different from Gong or Chorus?

They record calls and summarise them after the fact. PitchGenius tells you what to say next while the buyer is still in the room. That is the wedge: nobody else claims live in-conversation coaching.

What does it actually look like during a call?

A small card, always on top, floating over whatever video call you're already in — Zoom, Meet, Teams. It shows what the buyer is feeling, how ready they are, and the sentence to say next. It is a desktop app, not a Chrome extension, not a post-call report.
