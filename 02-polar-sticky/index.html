What is PitchGenius?

A real-time sales co-pilot. It listens to a live sales call and tells the rep what the buyer is feeling and the exact sentence to say next.

Is it a Chrome extension?

No. It ships as a desktop app: a small always-on-top card floating over the video call the rep is already in.

What do managers see?

Managers see usage and deal activity. Rep calibration answers are private.

Does it replace the rep?

No. PitchGenius makes the salesperson better at the human part of selling. It gives the rep more context while the conversation is still alive.

What happens after the call?

The next call starts with evidence-linked analysis, a written Buyer Readiness Index™ verdict, MEDDPICC gaps, and recommended next moves.
