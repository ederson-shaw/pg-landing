# Variant G

G is a new static landing page built from the editorial language of `pitchgenius_website.zip` and the 16-page preview set, with a distinct “signal trace” treatment. The page keeps the approved PitchGenius product story and uses the real HumanCue moment for Russell Pontone: 45 · DISCOVERY, the screen-display observation, the approved next move, sentence, and why-now line.

## Direction

- Display and body: self-hosted Satoshi, with a lighter display weight and a compact mono utility layer for evidence labels.
- Palette: `#081d31` ink for the decision field, `#fbfcfd` paper for editorial sections, `#eef6f8` evidence surfaces, `#18d9e6` for a live signal, `#007b91` for readable cyan text, and `#d4514c` for risk.
- Signature: one evidence trace travels from the hero through the evidence orbit, HumanCue wave, and intelligence loop; it is the page’s visual proof that a quiet CRM record and a changing buyer can be different things.
- The handmade layer is `assets/g-ink.svg`, a restrained irregular underline beside the hero thesis.

## Honest limitation

The HumanCue panel is a faithful static demonstration of the approved live moment; this static deliverable does not connect to a live call or backend state. The “Book a pilot” CTA therefore remains an external link to `pitchgenius.ai`.
