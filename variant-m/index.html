HumanCue™

Then the call starts.

And everything can change.

HumanCue speaks only when something matters.

Most live AI gives the salesperson more information to process. HumanCue is designed to do the opposite. It listens for decision-relevant change and asks:

  • Did something meaningful happen?
  • Is there enough evidence?
  • Does it matter now?
  • Can the rep do something useful about it?

If the answer is no — HumanCue stays quiet.

Buyer Readiness Index™

A deal can move stages without the buyer moving at all.

Sales systems measure activity. Calls. Meetings. Emails. Stages. Forecasts. PitchGenius asks a different question:

Is the buyer actually becoming ready to decide?

  1. Trust

    Are they increasingly willing to engage honestly?

  2. Confidence

    Do they believe enough in the direction to continue?

  3. Urgency

    Is there a reason to solve this now?

  4. Emotional safety

    Can the buyer discuss risk, concerns, and consequences openly?

  5. Perceived risk

    Is risk becoming resolved — or increasing?

  6. Willingness to move

    Is the buyer investing real effort into the next decision step?
