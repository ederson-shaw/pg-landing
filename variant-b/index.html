Pre-Call Buyer Intelligence™

Walk into the call knowing more than the account.

Your CRM can tell you the stage. LinkedIn can tell you the title. Previous calls can tell you what was discussed. PitchGenius helps answer a harder question:

How should I approach this buyer?

Using the evidence available around the opportunity, PitchGenius builds a working picture of the buyer, the decision, the risks, and what the rep still needs to learn.

Buyer Readiness Index™

A deal can move stages without the buyer moving at all.

Sales systems measure activity. Calls. Meetings. Emails. Stages. Forecasts. PitchGenius asks a different question:

Is the buyer actually becoming ready to decide?

  1. Trust

    Are they increasingly willing to engage honestly?

  2. Confidence

    Do they believe enough in the direction to continue?

  3. Urgency

    Is there a reason to solve this now?

  4. Perceived risk

    What still makes the decision feel unsafe?

  5. Willingness to move

    Are their actions consistent with advancement?

  6. Decision clarity

    Do they understand enough to make the next decision?

Do not ask: “Will it close?”

Ask: “What still has to happen before this buyer is ready?”

Two completely different truths.

CRM stages describe the seller’s process. Buyer readiness describes the buyer’s decision. Those two things are not always aligned.

Your CRM knows where the deal is. PitchGenius helps you understand where the buyer is.
