For the manager

The arithmetic, without a single number we can’t prove.

We don’t have published win-rate data yet. So here is the mechanism, in the language of the person who signs.

i — Win rate

Same reps, more closes.

The same pipeline, the same headcount — reps close more because they say the right thing at the right moment instead of finding out what went wrong three weeks later.

ii — Ramp time

Weeks, not quarters.

A new rep performs like a good rep sooner, because the coaching happens during the call instead of in a Monday review of calls that are already over.

iii — Forecast accuracy

Readiness, not hope.

Buyer readiness is scored on every call, so the pipeline stops being what reps hope and starts being what the buyers actually showed.

iv — Coaching capacity

All forty calls, not one.

One manager cannot sit on forty calls a week. The co-pilot sits on all of them, and surfaces the two that need a human.
