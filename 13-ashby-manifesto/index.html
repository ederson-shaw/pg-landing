What this is not

It does not sell for you.

The rep stays the hero. PitchGenius is the guide — calm, specific, never boastful. It does not speak for you. It hands you the line, and you decide.

Calibration is ten questions, answered once, that tune how the co-pilot talks to the rep. Those answers are private. Managers see usage and deal activity. They do not see the rep's answers. The product ships with that promise.

What this is

A guide that sits on every call.

It listens. It reads the room. It tells you what the buyer is feeling as they feel it. It gives you the exact sentence to say next — and the reason it is the right sentence, right now.

It is a desktop app, a small always-on-top card floating over whatever video call the rep is already in. Zoom, Meet, Teams. Not a Chrome extension. Not a post-call report tool.

Every closed contract you make as a seller is another piece of life you are building for your family.
