READINESS VS. DEAL STAGE

SELLER PROCESS ≠ BUYER DECISION

Same CRM stage.
Completely different deal.

  • Proposal delivered
  • Follow-up scheduled
  • Forecast positive
STATUSHealthy
vs.
BUYER REALITYEvidence review
  • Implementation risk unresolved
  • Economic buyer unknown
  • No buyer-owned next step
  • Willingness may have declined

CRM stages describe the seller’s process.
