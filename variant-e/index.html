Managers cannot attend every conversation. PitchGenius combines call evidence, HumanCue interventions, buyer-readiness movement, MEDDPICC evidence, and seller behavior to reveal patterns worth coaching.

THE MANAGEMENT MATH Better seller judgment can help the same team close more of the pipeline, shorten ramp through live reinforcement, add buyer evidence to forecasts, and extend coaching beyond the calls one manager can attend.

SELLER JUDGMENT REVIEWFOCUS / RECURRING PATTERN

WHAT CHANGED

Implementation concern surfaced after an otherwise positive proposal conversation.

WHAT THE REP SAW

Buyer’s request to slow the rollout discussion.

WHAT THE REP MISSED

The buyer returned to risk twice, but the response moved back to feature explanation.

WHAT BLOCKED MOVEMENT

No shared view of ownership or a safe implementation path.

WHAT TO COACH

Explore perceived risk before explaining; test the missing stakeholder.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Use this moment as a concrete rehearsal before the next executive call.

These are not isolated script mistakes. They are judgment patterns.
